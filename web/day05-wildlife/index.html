What this shows. Records reported to GBIF by participating publishers. Counts are occurrence records, not animals, species abundance or your chance of a sighting. Recording effort, seasonality and the source datasets vary. Some records have deliberately generalised locations or large coordinate uncertainty. Map markers represent published coordinates, not guaranteed exact sightings.

Default observation years: 2021–2025. The map displays a sample of the matching records, labelled with its size, while the field-guide counts come from the GBIF species facet over the full filtered search. Exact source records and licences are linked from map pop-ups.
