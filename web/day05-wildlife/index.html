About these records. Records reported to GBIF by participating publishers. NBN Atlas has additional UK records, but these are not included here until their separate licences, location protections and duplication can be handled properly. Counts are occurrence records, not animals, species abundance or your chance of a sighting. Recording effort, seasonality and the source datasets vary. Some records have deliberately generalised locations or large coordinate uncertainty. Map markers represent published coordinates, not guaranteed exact sightings.

Default observation years: 2021–2025. The map displays a sample of the matching records, labelled with its size, while the field-guide counts come from the GBIF species facet over the full filtered search. Species photographs are drawn from separately licensed GBIF media records and are not evidence of a local sighting. Exact source records and licences are linked from map pop-ups.

Data © contributing GBIF publishers, subject to each record’s licence · Map © OpenStreetMap contributors · Place search © OpenStreetMap / Nominatim.
