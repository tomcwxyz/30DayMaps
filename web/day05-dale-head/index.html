The question

How does a mountain change what we see?

This first study reconstructs the topography around Dale Head using published elevation tiles. Change direction or leave the summit to explore the ridges and valleys from above. This is a terrain model, not a photograph or a claim to recreate trees, buildings or atmospheric conditions.

Reading the map

Terrain first, then visibility

The summit camera is an experimental low-level terrain view. Relief comes from actual digital elevation data, but the result is not a surveyed optical panorama. A proper line-of-sight viewshed layer must be computed and validated separately, and is not yet shown in this version.

On touch screens, drag to look around. Use the direction buttons to return to a known heading, or switch to 3D overview for a conventional map.
