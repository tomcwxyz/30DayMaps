Under the lens

Select a moss cluster on the map to discover which species have been recorded there.
Shapes are recording clusters, not moss cover · 10 km source resolution
How it works. Neighbouring 10 km recording squares are grouped when they pass species and observation thresholds. At least three touching squares form a cluster. The softly rounded outlines are illustrative, not habitat boundaries. Gaps may mean nobody recorded moss.
