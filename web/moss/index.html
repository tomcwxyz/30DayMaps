Under the lens

Select a moss cluster on the map to discover which species have been recorded there.
Each coloured square contains a record from our eight-species dataset · 10 km source resolution
How it works. “All moss records” shows every source square containing at least one of eight selected species. “All 8 recorded together” shows strict eight-species co-occurrence. Habitat-group views show only clusters of concentrated recordings, formed when neighbouring 10 km squares pass species and record-count thresholds. Turn on “Reveal recorded squares outside clusters” to inspect the observations that do not meet those rules. Missing a coloured cluster is never evidence that a species is absent. The shapes are recording patterns, not moss cover.
