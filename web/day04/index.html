An atlas of collective movement

Where the sky gathers.

Each winter, thousands of starlings gather into restless, shifting clouds before disappearing into the reeds. The spectacle is called a murmuration.

    03 / WHY IT MATTERS

    Extraordinary together.
    Vulnerable apart.

    Starlings remain widespread but are Red-listed in the UK after a long-term population decline. A spectacular gathering can conceal a troubling trend.

    Places to feed, nest and shelter matter throughout the year, long after the winter display has ended.

    04 / SMALL ACTIONS

    Make room for starlings.

    You don't need a reedbed or a thousand birds. A garden, a roofline and a few neighbours can help.

    Going to watch? Arrive before dusk, keep your distance, stay on paths and never disturb birds at the roost. Check recent site information first: roosts can move, and some locations are deliberately not publicised.
