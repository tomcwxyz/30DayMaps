Green: known public access to woodland · Ochre: no known public access · Dashed orange: hypothetical link. Map layers update as you pan or zoom.

This is a thought experiment, not an access map or planning proposal. “No known public access” does not mean legally closed, and “public access” does not guarantee access or accessibility at any particular entrance. The woodland layer also includes narrow corridors along public paths; it must not be read as access to an entire woodland. The 800 m circle is a straight-line illustration: no road/path routing, entrances, barriers, slope, population distribution, habitat quality or landowner consent has been assessed. Hypothetical links are straight lines, not proposed footpaths. No statement about “everyone” or regional coverage is calculated here.
